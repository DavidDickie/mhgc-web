Mega Hyper Galactic Conquest

The 1994 play-by-email space conquest game, now in your browser.

Games

    How it works

    • A game has 4, 8, 9, 12, 16, 20 or 25 empires. Seats not taken by humans are computer empires.
    • Pick a game, then log in to your race with its password, or take an open seat and choose one. You can play from any browser.
    • One person may play several races; switch between them in the top bar.
    • The turn runs when every human has submitted. The host can also force it.
    • No deadlines: a game waits as long as it takes.

    Host controls

    Everyone sees a notice in the chat when you reset a password or change who plays a race. Every host action is kept in the host audit log, which the export includes.

    Reset password

    Give the race a temporary password and tell its player. Every browser logged in to it is logged out, and the player must choose a new password at the next login.
